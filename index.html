BARBELL BRAWL

Lift. Level. Brawl. The crew that lifts together, fights together. These are rough, throwaway prototypes, not the real app.

Realtime Brawl Play this one

Stick fighters vs Zone 1: the excuse swarm (Snack Attackers, Couch Potatoes, Snooze Imps), the Bridge Troll, and Oni Bro. On a phone: tap Play fullscreen and turn sideways: left thumb moves, big JUMP bottom-right, and three control layouts to compare. Install it from the browser menu (Add to Home screen) and it opens straight into the game. On a laptop: two players can share the keyboard. Red means an attack is coming; purple can't be blocked, so dodge.

Workout Tracker

The workout-logging side, built for phone width: sets, warmups, rep ranges, rest timers, quick-logged runs, PRs, weekly streaks, and your fighter's level and stats. Try Settings → Load 8 weeks of sample workouts. Data stays in your own browser.

Balance Sim

For the number nerds: simulated players training for weeks, showing how XP, levels and stats come out, plus a couch-to-5K vs 30 mi/week fairness check.
