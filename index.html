BARBELL BRAWL

Lift with your friends. Then fight them… well, fight monsters together. These are rough, throwaway prototypes, not the real app.

Realtime Brawl Play this one

Stick fighter vs a monster, solo or co-op. On a phone: use the on-screen buttons (CPU buddy as Player 2). On a laptop: two players share the keyboard (set Player 2 to Human). Red means an attack is coming: jump it, duck it, dodge it, or block it.

Workout Tracker

The workout-logging side, built for phone width. Try Settings → Load 8 weeks of sample workouts, then open Progress. Data stays in your own browser.

Art Mockups
