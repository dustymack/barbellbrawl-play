Tradeoffs

A · Stick-fight
  • Fastest to build and animate: poses are just joint positions, so new moves and gear are cheap.
  • Matches the tracker's exercise art already.
  • Swole comedy reads well (bulging arms on a stick body).
  • Risk: less "product" feel. The anime FX carry most of the excitement.
B · 16-bit SNES
  • Instant nostalgia and personality. It feels like a real fighting game.
  • Gear, outfits and cosmetics shine (skins, headbands, colors), which suits rewards.
  • Risk: more art effort per animation frame. A hybrid could work: generate sprites from a skeleton like this mockup, and hand-polish key frames.
  • The tracker UI could stay clean and modern, with pixel art only in the arena.

What to react to

  1. Fusion: 16-bit arena or clean dark arena behind the stick heroes?
  2. Hero identity: headband and aura in the player's color, plus hair (spiky, ponytail, bun, buzz). Enough to tell four friends apart?
  3. How swole at level 50: this much, more, or less? Keep the tiny head joke?
  4. Favorite gear so far? Any gym items you'd love as weapons or armor?
  5. How much anime: aura and speed lines only, or also spiky hair, headband and impact text?
