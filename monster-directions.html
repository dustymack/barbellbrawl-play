The monsters in the chosen direction, C · Chibi Mana (founder, 2026-09-25), then the comparison that picked it: the Zone 1 lineup as it was, and three 16-bit directions on a better pixel pipeline.

Why the current ones read as blobs
  • No interior lines. Arm, torso and head are one color, so the Oni's flexing arm melts into its body.
  • Shading is one dark ellipse stuck on the side. It reads as a hole or a stain, not as a round shape.
  • Faces are 1–2 pixels. The face is how you tell what a creature is, and it's the first thing lost.
  • Black outline on a dark arena. The silhouette edge disappears. A rim light (or a lighter background) brings it back.

×2 is roughly minion size on a phone mid-fight. Silhouette test: if you can still tell what it is as a flat shape, it'll read in a hectic fight.
