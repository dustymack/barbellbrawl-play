YOUR FIGHTER AROUND THE APP

Throwaway prototype. Your stick fighter lives on the home screen and in the loading animations, drawn live from your level, hair and color (not a GIF), so it grows as you train. Tap a fighter for a reaction. Everything here uses the same skeleton as the art mockups.

1 · Home screen: three placements

Same fighter, three amounts of screen. The Start button never moves and the fighter never delays it. "You train like a…" changes which idle moves come up.

A · Hero card

Your fighter is the top of the home screen, with level and XP. The biggest "this is a game" signal, at the cost of about a third of the screen.

2 · Loading animations

Your own fighter, looping one rep. The line under each is a loading joke (the tone doc allows jokes in loading lines, never in the way of logging).

Size:

In a button

Tap Finish. The loader only shows if saving takes over 300 ms, so fast saves never flash it.

Progress: the sled push

For long jobs with a known progress, like importing history. Your fighter pushes the bar full.

Pull to refresh: pull-ups

Drag the list down. Pull far enough and your fighter gets a chin over the bar; let go to refresh.
